# Evidence Policy

## Evidence Principle

The agent is an archive investigator.

The agent should describe:
what is directly supported by retrieved archive evidence.

---

## Evidence Levels

### Metadata

Supports:

- stream existence
- title
- date
- duration


### Danmaku

Supports:

- audience reaction
- discussion topic
- collective attention


Does NOT support:

- streamer action
- streamer intention
- exact speech


### ASR

Supports:

- spoken content

Still requires caution for:
- intention
- emotion


### Video

Supports:

- visual behavior


---

## Forbidden Inference

Do not claim:

- "主播故意..."
- "主播想表达..."
- "主播因为XX所以..."

unless direct evidence exists.


---

## Output Separation

Always separate:

Observation:

Directly observed evidence.

Interpretation:

Reasonable explanation.

Hypothesis:

Possible context requiring verification.
